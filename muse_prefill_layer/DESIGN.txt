muse_prefill_layer
==================

One transformer layer step over a prefill batch. Every stage is one launch or a few, in data-dependency order.

Pipeline
--------
  1  QKV projection        cuBLASLt with an algorithm pinned per shape in prepare (else torch.mm)
  2  Q/K prep              RMS-norm of Q and K, RoPE, FP8 Q, K / V into the paged cache (CUDA kernel; Triton twin as
                           fallback)
  3  attention             warp-specialized FP8 FMHA (PV and the softmax row sum as one m64n72k32 wgmma), the
                           attn_fwd kernel, or FA3 + fused norm * sigmoid(gate); chosen per shape class after
                           validation
  4  o projection          then boundary 1: stream = alpha * stream + beta * rms(branch), next input
                           rms(stream) * (1 + g_pre); also zeroes the MoE work counters
  5  router + pooler       router GEMM and top-8 on a side stream, pooler GEMM on the main stream
  6  grouped MoE           gather with the pre-expert norm into per-expert rows; one persistent kernel runs both
                           expert GEMMs with NVFP4 dequantized in registers as the wgmma A operand; SiLU * up fused
  7  combine + unpooler    per-route rows combined with per-expert RMS-norm and routing weights, unpooler GEMM,
                           boundary 2 (large T rebuilds the fp32 stream from boundary 1's saved RMS factor)

prepare builds the extensions, repacks the NVFP4 experts, and tunes the cuBLASLt algorithm per GEMM and the
attention variant per shape class; nothing else carries over between calls.

Sections (py/)
--------------
  00_layer        geometry, Triton kernels (QK prep, norm-gate, boundary, combine), prepare
  01_dense_lt     cuBLASLt pinning and tuning, GEMM dispatch
  02_moe_pack     NVFP4 code / scale repacking
  03_attention    attention paths, validation, per-shape-class choice
  04_moe_forward  MoE launch sequence, QK-prep and attention runners
  05_fmha         FMHA build, split pick, launch, prepare
  06_kernel_fn    per-thread CUDA context binding, kernel_fn
  07_selftest     self-tests (check runs only)
  08_build        extension builds, source references

CUDA sources (cuda/)
--------------------
  prefill.cu            expert GEMMs (fused persistent kernel), routing plan, gather, route, combine, Q/K prep,
                        boundaries, attention (attn_fwd, stream-K variant)
  fmha.cu               FP8 prefill FMHA and K/V gather
  glue.cu               host launchers (programmatic dependent launch)
  prefill_bindings.cpp  torch bindings
  lt_glue.cpp           cuBLASLt wrapper with a pinned algorithm config
